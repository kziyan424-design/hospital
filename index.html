Helpful information

Frequently asked questions.

For current timings, fees, doctor availability and services, please contact the hospital directly.

How do I request an appointment?

Complete the enquiry form on this page. It saves a local demo record and prepares a WhatsApp message. An appointment is not confirmed until the hospital confirms it.

Are all listed services available?

Service cards are informational placeholders. Please contact the hospital to verify actual services, facilities and operating hours.

Can I use the website on a mobile?

Yes. The layout adapts to mobile phones, tablets, laptops and desktop screens.

Does the admin panel work across devices?

No. This single-file demo uses local browser storage. A secure shared database and server-side login are required for multiple devices.
